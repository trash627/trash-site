Safety-Critical Control Synthesis for Robotic Systems

February 15, 2026 · 136 words · Me

Overview#

Robotic systems increasingly operate in unstructured environments alongside humans. This project develops control synthesis methods that provide formal safety guarantees while maintaining performance and adaptability.

Background#

Traditional robot control relies on careful tuning and extensive testing. However, this approach cannot guarantee safety when robots encounter novel situations or unexpected disturbances.

Control barrier functions and control Lyapunov functions offer a principled approach to safety, but synthesizing these functions automatically remains challenging.

Approach#

Key technical directions include:

  • Control barrier function synthesis using sum-of-squares optimization
  • Learning-based adaptation with formal safety shields
  • Compositional synthesis for high-degree-of-freedom systems
  • Runtime monitoring with formal guarantees

Results#

  • Safe walking gaits for bipedal robots under terrain uncertainty
  • Collision-free manipulation in cluttered environments
  • Integration with ROS 2 for real-world deployment

Future Work#

Future work will address multi-robot coordination and human-robot interaction with formal safety guarantees.

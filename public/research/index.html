Research

My research broadly covers developing formal logical methods for the verification of continuous and hybrid dynamical systems. In particular, I’m interested in sound and complete axiomatizations of properties of ordinary differential equations (ODEs) in differential dynamic logic (dL). I’m also interested in developing sound and efficient methods for invariant synthesis and methods for deciding nonlinear real arithmetic, which make hyrbid systems theorem proving more tractable in practice.

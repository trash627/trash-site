Differential Dynamic Logic for Hybrid Systems Verification

May 15, 2026 · 176 words · Me

Overview#

This project focuses on advancing the state-of-the-art in formal verification of hybrid systems—systems that combine discrete control with continuous dynamics. We develop new proof calculi and automation strategies within the framework of differential dynamic logic (dL).

Background#

Hybrid systems are ubiquitous in safety-critical applications such as autonomous vehicles, medical devices, and air traffic control. Ensuring their safety requires rigorous mathematical proofs that account for both discrete mode switches and continuous evolution governed by differential equations.

Differential dynamic logic provides a complete axiomatization for reasoning about hybrid systems, but practical verification still requires significant manual effort.

Approach#

Our research develops:

  • New proof rules for complex continuous dynamics including non-linear ODEs
  • Automation strategies using machine learning-guided proof search
  • Compositional verification techniques for large-scale systems
  • Integration with SMT solvers for efficient arithmetic reasoning

Results#

  • Novel induction principles for liveness properties of hybrid systems
  • Automated invariant generation for polynomial vector fields
  • Case studies on automotive controllers and robotic systems

Future Work#

We plan to extend our techniques to stochastic hybrid systems and develop better integration with industrial verification tools.

Promit Panja

I am a second-year PhD student in Computer Science at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), Germany, and part of the Logic of Autonomous Dynamical Systems research group advised by Prof. André Platzer. My current research involves devoloping logical methods for proving safety of continuous and hybdrid dynamical systems.

Prior to joining KIT, I was a master’s student at Virginia Tech, USA, primarily researching nonlinear control theory and safety-critial robotics.

Correct-by-Construction Barrier Certificate Synthesis

Overview Autonomous driving presents unique challenges for formal verification due to the complexity of the environment, interaction with human drivers, and stringent safety requirements. This project develops domain-specific logical frameworks tailored to autonomous driving scenarios. Background Self-driving cars must navigate complex traffic scenarios while guaranteeing safety. Traditional testing and simulation cannot provide the exhaustive coverage needed for safety certification. Formal methods offer a path to rigorous safety guarantees. However, existing formalisms struggle with the richness of driving scenarios, including continuous vehicle dynamics, discrete decision-making, and multi-agent interactions. ...
